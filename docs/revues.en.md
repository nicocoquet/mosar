# Journals { .page-title-compact }

<div class="revues-header">
  <div class="revues-intro">
<p>The journals surveyed as part of the <strong>Mosar</strong> project are presented here.</p>
<p>This survey draws on data from <strong>Mir@bel</strong>, a shared reference database to which Mosar contributes by adding journals and completing or correcting metadata. This information is then retrieved and cross-referenced with the analytical data collected specifically for the Mosar project. The explorer allows users to filter journals by combining criteria relating to their scientific and editorial characteristics and modes of dissemination.</p>

<div class="revues-levels">
<h3>Levels of proximity</h3>
<p>Four levels of proximity are used to characterise the relationships between the journals and the institutions covered by the project.</p>
<ul>
<li><span class="revues-level-badge">Level 1</span><div class="revues-level-description"><strong class="revues-level-title">Publishing:</strong> journals published by MSH Mondes, Presses universitaires de Nanterre or Éditions de la Sorbonne.</div></li>
<li><span class="revues-level-badge">Level 2</span><div class="revues-level-description"><strong class="revues-level-title">Research laboratories:</strong> journals managed or hosted by a research laboratory affiliated with Université Paris Nanterre or Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne.</div></li>
<li><span class="revues-level-badge">Level 3</span><div class="revues-level-description"><strong class="revues-level-title">Editorial responsibilities or support:</strong> journals whose editor-in-chief is affiliated with either university, whose core editorial board includes at least one member of either institution, or which receive financial support from a research unit.</div></li>
<li><span class="revues-level-badge">Level 4</span><div class="revues-level-description"><strong class="revues-level-title">Committee membership:</strong> journals with at least one member of their editorial board or scientific advisory board affiliated with either university.</div></li>
</ul>
