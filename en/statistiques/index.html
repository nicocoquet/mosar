    Statistics

    This page brings together the main indicators produced from the Mosar journal survey. The charts explore the composition of the corpus, the journals’ editorial characteristics and their dissemination models.

    Distribution of journals by degree of proximity to Paris Nanterre University, Paris 1 University and MSH Mondes

    Level 1 : journals published by MSH Mondes, Presses universitaires de Nanterre or Éditions de la Sorbonne

    Level 2 : journals hosted or supported by a research unit affiliated with Paris 1 or Paris Nanterre University

    Level 3 : journals whose editor-in-chief is affiliated with Paris 1 or Paris Nanterre University, with at least one Paris 1 or Paris Nanterre member on a small editorial board (≤ 8), or with financial support from a research unit

    Level 4 : journals with at least one member affiliated with Paris 1 or Paris Nanterre University on the editorial or scientific board
