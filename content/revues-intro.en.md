This page presents the journals surveyed as part of the **Mosar** project, within the institutional scope of Université Paris Nanterre and Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne. It provides an opportunity to explore this publishing landscape through its scientific, editorial and dissemination characteristics.

The survey draws on data from **Mir@bel**, a shared journal information resource to which Mosar contributes by adding journals and completing or correcting metadata. This information is then retrieved and combined with analytical data produced by the Mosar project. The explorer allows users to search for journals and combine different criteria using the available filters.

### Levels of proximity

Four levels of proximity describe the relationships between the journals and the institutions concerned:

- **Level 1 — Publishing:** journals published by MSH Mondes, Presses universitaires de Nanterre or Éditions de la Sorbonne.
- **Level 2 — Research units:** journals supported or hosted by a research unit affiliated with Paris 1 or Paris Nanterre University.
- **Level 3 — Editorial responsibilities or support:** journals whose editor-in-chief is affiliated with either university, whose small editorial board (no more than eight members) includes at least one member affiliated with either institution, or which receive financial support from a research unit.
- **Level 4 — Editorial or scientific board membership:** journals with at least one member of their editorial or scientific board affiliated with either university.
